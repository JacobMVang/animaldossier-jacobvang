The Southern White Rhinoceras

The southern white rhinoceros or southern white rhino (Ceratotherium simum simum)
is one of the two subspecies of the white rhinoceros (the other being the much rarer northern white rhinoceros).
It is the most common and widespread subspecies of rhinoceros.
