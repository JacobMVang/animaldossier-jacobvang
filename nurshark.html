The Nurse Shark

Basic Information

The nurse shark is a bottom-dwelling shark found in the western Atlantic Ocean and the eastern Pacific Ocean. It is a slow-moving, docile species that feeds on small fish, crustaceans, and mollusks.

Habitat & Geography

The nurse shark is found in the coastal waters of the Atlantic Ocean, in Europe, Africa, and the Americas.

Ecology & Behavior

  • Diet: Bottom-dwelling invertebrates and fish
  • Activity: Nocturnal
  • Reproduction: Gestation lasts for 6 months; broods contain 20-50 pups
  • Lifespan: Up to 20 years
  • Social Structure: Solitary or in small groups
  • Communication: Vibrations and chemical signals
  • Territorial Behavior: Defends territory against conspecifics
  • Predators: Large sharks and marine mammals
